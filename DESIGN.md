## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an ultra-premium, institutional-grade digital interface for global physical commodities, metals trading, and industrial circularity operations. The design aesthetic fuses strict International Typographic Style (Swiss Modernism) with high-precision enterprise mechanics. It projects unyielding stability, absolute transparency, and architectural discipline.

Targeting institutional procurement directors, global foundry suppliers, scrap yard consolidators, and ESG audit executives, the UI avoids superficial SaaS trends, skeuomorphic noise, or whimsical patterns. Instead, it relies on pristine white expanses, structural micro-borders, dense data legibility, and warm copper metallic moments reminiscent of refined industrial metallurgy (smelted copper, cathode sheets, and structural raw alloys).

The emotional outcome is crystalline confidence, sovereign enterprise reliability, and mathematical order across multi-million-dollar commodity lots and supply chain audits.

## Colors

The palette leverages a pristine, clinical white foundation interrupted only by structural graphite typography, metallurgical copper accents, and circularity emerald highlights.

- **Canvas & Surface Tier**: Primary background is pure stark white (`#ffffff`). Layered surfaces and nested card compartments use surgical slate tints (`#f8fafc` for outer containers, `#f1f5f9` for internal interactive recesses and data headers).
- **Graphite & Obsidian Monoliths (Typography)**: Deep slate obsidian (`#0f172a`) anchors primary headings and trade tickers; dark graphite (`#1e293b`) renders critical values and interactive states; slate neutral (`#334155` and `#64748b`) provides secondary meta-labels.
- **Metallurgical Accents (Copper & Bronze)**: Primary accent is unrefined raw copper (`#b45309`), expanding into molten amber (`#d97706`) for interactive hovers and burnished bronze (`#c2410c`) for active confirmations and spot price highlights.
- **Circularity Metric (Emerald)**: Reserved specifically for scrap recovery ratios, circular economy yield, purity verification, and ESG net-neutral benchmarks (`#059669`).
- **Structural Boundary**: Hairline separation borders use crisp cold slate (`#e2e8f0`), ensuring clean boundary definition without visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends architectural Swiss precision with engineering telemetry. 

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk)**: Structural, geometric, and authoritative. Headlines should feature tight letter tracking and crisp horizontal termination. All headers maintain high contrast (`#0f172a`) on light surfaces.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Hanken Grotesk)**: An ultra-clean contemporary neo-grotesque that maintains neutrality and legibility across dense specification sheets, bills of lading, and metallurgical certifications.
- **Telemetry, Metrics & Data (JetBrains Mono)**: Reserved for scrap tonnage counts, lot numbers, LME spot indices, elemental purity grades (e.g., Cu 99.99%), and audit timestamps. Sets an unapologetically rigorous industrial tone.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column mathematical grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single- or dual-column structural flow on mobile. Layout structures reflect industrial blueprint schematics: sharp, aligned, and disciplined.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (<768px), Tablet (768px–1199px), Desktop (1200px+), Ultra-Wide Trading Terminal (1600px+).
- **Margins & Gutters**: Generous outer margins on wide viewports (48px / 3rem) ensure that dense ledger tables and transactional monitors do not touch the screen perimeter. Column gutters scale from 12px on mobile to 24px on desktop.
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing strictly adheres to 4px and 8px base units. Internal card paddings use `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile. Data cell padding is compressed (`space-xs` vertically, `space-sm` horizontally) to maximize dense terminal throughput.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **tonal structural layering** and **hairline boundaries** rather than standard diffuse drop shadows. This avoids visual fatigue in high-frequency trading and logistics environments.

- **Base Layer (L0)**: `#ffffff` canvas. Represents unassigned workspace and baseline layout background.
- **Container Layer (L1)**: `#f8fafc` panels with a sharp 1px border of `#e2e8f0`. No elevation shadow. This creates crisp boundaries reminiscent of architectural technical drafting.
- **Interactive Floating Layer (L2)**: Context menus, modal sheets, and quick-trade flyouts retain the `#ffffff` fill, paired with an ultra-fine, razor-thin perimeter border (`#cbd5e1`) and an ambient, low-dispersion drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active State Depth**: Active inputs and selected inventory items use an inner metallic micro-glow or a clean 1px solid copper ring (`#b45309`) with zero blur, avoiding fuzzy halos.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained **Soft (Level 1)** corner curvature. Radii are kept intentionally minimal (`0.25rem` / 4px on standard elements, `0.375rem` / 6px on modal panels and larger dashboard cards) to echo the precise shearing and machined edges of heavy industrial metal sheets.

- Pills and large bubble curves are completely avoided for primary actions, structural cards, and data tables.
- A single functional exception is made for round scrap status indicators and elemental isotope badges, which leverage strict mathematical circles (`50%` border-radius) to indicate operational equilibrium and closed-loop recyclability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Transactional / Execute)**: Solid copper background (`#b45309`), stark white text (`#ffffff`), font weight 600, radius 4px, height 40px (desktop) / 44px (mobile touch target). On hover: shifts to molten amber (`#d97706`). On press: drops to burnished bronze (`#c2410c`).
- **Secondary (Structural / Action)**: Deep obsidian border (`1px solid #0f172a`), transparent background, `#0f172a` text. On hover: solid `#0f172a` background with white text.
- **Tertiary / Neutral**: Light slate container (`#f1f5f9`), slate text (`#334155`), border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Hover turns background to `#e2e8f0`.

### Data Cards & Scrap Lot Modules
- **Outer Shell**: Fill `#f8fafc`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, radius 6px.
- **Internal Header**: Border-bottom `1px solid #e2e8f0`, padding 12px 16px, background `#ffffff`.
- **Key Metrics Grid**: Two or three column internal blocks showing Lot ID (Mono Sm), Metal Category, Gross Mass (MT), and Yield Rate.

### Chips & Badges
- **Circularity / Recovery Badges**: Background `#ecfdf5`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`, text `#059669`. Uses `label-mono-sm` for uppercase specification (e.g., `RECYCLED 94.2%`).
- **Metal Grade Indicators**: Background `#fef3c7`, border `1px solid #fde68a`, text `#92400e`.
- **Lot Status (Inbound/Smelted)**: Background `#f1f5f9`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, text `#475569`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height 40px, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, radius 4px.
- Typographic style is `body-md` in `#0f172a`. Floating or top labels use `label-mono-sm` in `#64748b`.
- **Focus State**: Border color switches instantly to `#b45309` with a crisp 1px outline offset.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px x 16px with 2px corner radius for checkboxes; 16px circular for radio buttons.
- Inactive border: `1.5px solid #94a3b8`. Checked: solid `#0f172a` (or `#b45309` when selecting transaction lots) with a crisp white check glyph.

### Tables & Data Grids (Industrial Ledger)
- Row height: 44px dense, 52px comfortable.
- Header row: `#f8fafc`, typography `label-mono-sm` uppercase in `#475569`, border-bottom `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`.
- Row hover: `#f8fafc` background transition. Numeric cells align right using `label-mono-md`.
- Alternating rows remain crisp white (`#ffffff`) separated by `1px solid #f1f5f9`.
